## Part 1: Prompt Design & Iteration

### 1.1 Scenario A: Technical Explanation

**Prompt**

```text
Explain how neural networks work to a 10-year-old.
```

**Output**

```text
Imagine you're teaching a robot to recognize cats.

A neural network is like a giant team of tiny helpers inside a computer.

1. **Input Layer (The Eyes)**

   * The robot looks at a picture.
   * The picture is broken into lots of tiny pieces of information.

2. **Hidden Layers (The Thinkers)**

   * Each helper looks for something simple, like:

     * Is there a pointy shape?
     * Are there whiskers?
     * Are there two eyes?
   * They pass clues to other helpers.

3. **Output Layer (The Decision Maker)**

   * After all the helpers share clues, the robot makes a guess:

     * "I think this is a cat!"

4. **Learning from Mistakes**

   * If the robot guesses wrong, a teacher tells it the correct answer.
   * The helpers adjust how much they listen to each other.
   * After seeing many pictures, the robot gets better and better.

Think of a neural network like a huge group of friends solving a puzzle together. Each friend knows a little bit, and by working together they can recognize things, translate languages, and even chat with people.

```
